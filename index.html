Who

We're Papiss and Seauph, a DJ couple. Aquamini is our two star signs squashed together, Aquarius and Gemini.

Both DJs. Papiss also produces: he builds beats live on his MPC, mid-set. Seauph sings: real vocals over real records. The result is somewhere between a DJ set and a live show.
