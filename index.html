The duo

We're Papiss and Seauph, a couple and a DJ duo from Marbella. Aquamini is our two star signs squashed together, Aquarius and Gemini.

Papiss produces: he builds beats live on his MPC, mid-set. Seauph sings: real vocals over real records. The result is somewhere between a DJ set and a live show, and we've stopped trying to pick.
