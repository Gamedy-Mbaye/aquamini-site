Press

Aquamini is a DJ duo from Marbella, Spain, formed in 2025 by Papiss and Seauph. Papiss produces beats live on the MPC while Seauph adds layers of vocals, landing somewhere between a DJ set and a live performance. Their sound moves through soul, jazz, house and world music.

They founded Croissant Club, their own party in Marbella, hold a residency at Quinta Paisana, and have played across different venues in Europe.
